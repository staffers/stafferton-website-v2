Far more traffic for the money - next, more of the right traffic

On every efficiency measure Meta reports, AY 2025/26 was a big step up. Site clicks more than trebled on 59% more spend, cost per click more than halved, and click-through rate is well ahead of the real estate and all-industry benchmarks.

The focus for AY 2026/27 is turning that volume into bookings. Meta can now see booking, viewing and contact actions on the site, so campaigns can optimise for enquiries rather than clicks. Alongside that, fresher student-led creative and a bigger Instagram share will help the ads reach more prospective residents as well as the parents who already engage well.

Bookings & viewings from social
Live 12 Oct
Booking, viewing and contact events now sent to Meta

Through AY 2025/26 the campaigns were set to get as many clicks as possible. The Meta pixel recorded a general Lead event, but it could not tell a booking from an email click, so Meta had no clear booking signal to work towards. On 12 October 2026 we added separate Meta events for submitted bookings, viewing requests and contacts (form, phone and chat), and tightened the Lead event to genuine enquiries only. Once around a week of data has built up, the main campaign can move from clicks to optimising for leads, which is the single most valuable change for AY 2026/27.

Section 07

Recommendations for AY 2026/27

1
Optimise for enquiries now that Meta can see them

Booking, viewing and contact events have been passing to Meta since 12 October. Move the main campaign from link clicks to Leads optimisation once about a week of data has built up, then optimise for bookings when volumes reach around 50 a month.

From late OctHighest impact
2
Use creative to reach students, since targeting can't

Retire Video Tour 01 as the only ad. Build a rotation of student-voice, Instagram-native content: UGC in the style of the Eve videos, resident tours filmed on a phone, and boosts of the best organic posts. Format and tone are what bring the 18–24 audience back, as the AY 2024/25 Instagram boost showed (72% of spend reached 18–34s).

From NovNew creative every 8 weeks
3
Give Instagram its own budget and remove Audience Network

Run an Instagram Reels and Stories ad set with at least 30% of spend, and exclude Audience Network. This gives up some cheap clicks to get better ones, and should also lift the share of clicks that reach the page.

NovPlacement split
4
Launch Belfast

Belfast has never had paid social. Reuse the Sheffield campaign structure with Belfast creative on the £1,500 proposed in the AY 2026/27 media plan, with heavier spend in January to March and results week.

Dec£1,500 for the year
5
Retarget people who already know Liv

This year's 131,000 video ThruPlays and 22,000 landing page views make a large warm audience. Build video-viewer and site-visitor audiences and use them for booking-focused ads with prices and offers, especially around Clearing.

JanOnce audiences fill
6
Plan spend around the UCAS calendar

Line up social bursts with the UCAS Media programme (pre-results in May, results week in August) rather than spreading spend evenly at ~£450 a month. Keep a lower always-on level for the rest of the year.

May & AugFlighted
7
Check mobile landing page speed

With fewer than half of clicks becoming a page view, check the Sheffield and Belfast landing pages on mobile. Use a lighter, faster page for social traffic if needed.

Notes on the data

  • Source. Meta Ads Manager, Liv Student ad account, pulled 12 October 2026. Figures cover all campaigns in the account, which in both years promoted Sheffield only.
  • Periods. Academic years run 1 September to 31 August. AY 2024/25 had no activity from November 2024 to April 2025, so the May to August comparison is the fairer like-for-like view.
  • Site clicks are Meta link clicks. Landing page views require the Meta pixel to fire on livstudent.com, so they understate visits where cookies are declined.
  • Housing ads. All campaigns run under Meta's Housing special ad category, which blocks targeting by age, gender and postcode. The age split shows who Meta chose to deliver to, not who was targeted.
  • Benchmarks are US medians in dollars, converted at about $1 = £0.75, and come from the sources listed in Section 03.
  • Not included in this edition: organic post-level insights and Google Analytics on-site behaviour by social source.
