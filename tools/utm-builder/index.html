How to use the UTM builder

  1. Paste the page you want people to visit.
  2. Add the source, medium and campaign name, or start with a preset.
  3. Copy the finished URL into your ad, email or social post.
  4. Use the same naming pattern across campaigns to keep analytics tidy.

What the parameters mean

  • Source identifies where traffic came from, such as Google or a newsletter.
  • Medium identifies the channel, such as CPC, email or paid social.
  • Campaign groups links into the same marketing initiative.
  • Term and content optionally identify audiences, keywords and creative variants.
