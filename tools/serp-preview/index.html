What the preview tells you

        
It gives you a realistic desktop-style layout for drafting metadata. The colour, hierarchy and spacing help you judge whether the copy is clear and compelling.

      

      

        
Important limitation

        
Google can rewrite titles and descriptions and displays results differently by device and query. Character guidance is useful, but relevance and clarity matter more than hitting an exact count.
